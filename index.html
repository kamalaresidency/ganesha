Eco-Friendly Celebration Guidelines

Help us keep our celebration green, safe, clean, and joyous for everyone.

  • 🌱
    100% Eco-Friendly Clay Ganesha:

    Our society strictly adheres to non-toxic natural clay idols with organic turmeric & water-soluble colors.

  • 🚫
    Zero Single-Use Plastic:

    All prasad and meals are served in biodegradable areca leaf plates and eco-friendly compostable cups.

  • 🪔
    Fire & Electrical Safety:

    Aarti diyas and lighting fixtures are monitored by volunteer marshals to ensure child safety.

  • 🤝
    Voluntary Contributions:

    All donations are completely voluntary and openly recorded in our public Excel transparency ledger.
